How the Math Works

Concentration

Vial label = mg per 50 units. So each unit holds vial mg ÷ 50.

5 mg vial: 5 ÷ 50 = 0.1 mg per unit.

Units to mg

units × (vial mg ÷ 50)

37 units on a 5 mg vial: 37 × 0.1 = 3.7 mg.

mg to Units

target mg × 50 ÷ vial mg

3.7 mg on a 7.5 mg vial: 3.7 × 50 ÷ 7.5 = 24.7, draw 25 units.

Syringes are 100 units = 1 mL. Every vial is 2 mL (200 units). A dose under 50 units stretches the vial past 4 weeks; a dose over 50 units runs out before 4 weeks. Unit draws are rounded to the nearest whole unit, and the calculator shows the actual mg that rounded draw delivers.
